Run it yourself

One HTML file. Your keys stay in your browser.

No account and no server of ours. Most sources work with no setup. A free relay unlocks feeds that block browsers, and any AI you choose powers the analyst.

  1. 01
    Download the releaseOpen Looking Glass.html in any modern browser.
  2. 02
    Deploy the relayPaste looking-glass-worker.js into a free Cloudflare Worker. About five minutes.
  3. 03
    Connect an AIYour Anthropic key, any OpenAI-compatible service, or a free local model through Ollama.
